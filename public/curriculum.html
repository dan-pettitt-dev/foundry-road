Curriculum

We recognise the importance of play as a structured learning process and hope to provide every opportunity for children to learn through play in a happy, safe and relaxed atmosphere with companions of a similar age.

We aim to give the children the best start to their education by providing a broad curriculum including preschool focussed activities, (inc name writing, letter and number recognition, scissor and pencil control etc), PE sessions and much more which is all well planned, stimulating and purposeful and covers the seven areas of learning for their Foundation Stage and the characteristics of effective learning.

  1. Communication and Language
  2. Expressive Arts and Design
  3. Personal, Social and emotional Development
  4. Mathematics
  5. Understanding the World
  6. Physical Development
  7. Literacy

Children also engage in free play/choice from a huge range of activities and equipment.

We believe that children learn best when they are happy, safe and secure, and we aim to create a warm and welcoming environment where they can thrive.
