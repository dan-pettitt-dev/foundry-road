Opening Times and Info

We are open term time alongside the local schools.

  • Morning sessions: 9 am - 12 noon (Monday - Friday)
  • Afternoon sessions: 1 pm - 4 pm (Monday - Friday)
  • Full days: 9 am - 4 pm (Monday - Friday)
  • Lunch supervision (12 - 1 pm) available Monday - Friday

Funded children are entitled to 30 hours a week if eligible, which is equal to 5 full days (additional payment for non-funded hours required). When these are taken are for the Parents to decide, depending on availability. We can only claim for funding the term after they turn 3, or 2 if it's for Time for Two's. We also accept Time for Two's; please visit www.dudley.gov.uk/timefortwos to see if you qualify. We offer places for the additional 15 hours; to see if you qualify, please visit www.childcarechoices.gov.uk. Your child may also be eligible for the Early Years Pupil Premium, which is for the setting to use. To see if you qualify, please visit www.dudley.gov.uk/eypp and complete the short questionnaire.


Lunchbox Ideas

Prices

  • Full day 9-4pm - £40.00
  • Half a day either 9-12 or 1-4pm - £17.50 / £1.00 if funded
  • Half a day with additional hour supervision 9-1pm or 12-4pm - £23.00 / £7.50 if funded for the additional hour

We accept payment via the following options:

  • Cash
  • Direct bank payment / standing order
  • Some vouchers (please speak to management regarding this)

All fees are to be paid in advance on a weekly or monthly basis. All sessions are dependent on numbers, so please contact pre-school for further details.
