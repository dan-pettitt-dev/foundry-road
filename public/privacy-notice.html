Privacy Notice

Foundry Road Preschool takes your privacy seriously. In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation, we commit to the following:


Legal Basis for Collecting Data

  • Consent: The individual has given clear consent for us to process their personal data for a specific reason.
  • Contract: The processing is necessary for a contract we have with you, or because we have asked you to take specific steps before entering into a contract.
  • Legal obligation: The processing is necessary to comply with the law (not including contractual obligations).
  • Vital interests: The processing is necessary to protect someone’s life.
  • Public task: The processing is necessary for us to perform a task in the public interest or for official functions, and the task or function has a clear basis in law.
  • Legitimate interests: The processing is necessary for legitimate interests or the legitimate interests of a third party unless there is a good reason to protect the individual’s personal data which overrides those legitimate interests.

Information We Collect

  • Child’s name, date of birth, age, and address
  • Parents’ names, addresses, and contact numbers
  • Parental responsibility details
  • Emergency contact names, addresses, and numbers
  • Child’s doctor’s name and contact number
  • Health clinic/health visitor details
  • Child’s NHS number
  • Allergies, medical history, and immunisation details
  • Special educational needs or disabilities
  • Ethnic group, religion, and home language
  • Parents’ National Insurance number

How We Use Your Data

  • To support your child’s development
  • To monitor and report on your child’s progress
  • To share information about activities in our setting
  • To contact named people in an emergency
  • To share with other professionals in accordance with legislation
  • To ensure a contract of service is delivered and maintained
  • To ensure we receive the statutory funding for which we are eligible

Data Retention

All details will be destroyed following the required timeline of 7 years unless there are safeguarding referrals. In such cases, a copy will be sent to new settings and also kept with us until the child is 25 years old. They will then be destroyed accordingly.

Sharing Your Data

With your permission, this data may be shared with:

  • Other professionals supporting your child, such as health visitors, schools, or other educational professionals
  • The local authority regarding free childcare entitlement and the Early Years Census
  • The local safeguarding children’s board or Children’s Services Referral and Assessment Team if we have concerns about your child’s safety
  • Ofsted
