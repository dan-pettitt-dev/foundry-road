Workshops

We offer workshops where parents/carers or grandparents can come in and become involved in pre-school life. By joining in with activities and seeing what your child is doing whilst with us at pre-school, enabling you to get to know those who care for your children and for the team to get to know you. We have a lot of support and feel that these workshops are a great success. We hold them once a term, and give you notice dates and times, they will also be displayed on our diary dates. If we are holding a workshop on a day your child usually comes to pre-school and you are unable to come, please do not worry your child will still take part in the activity with a teacher.
